# Download protection

By default guests can only **view** photos. You decide per album whether they may also **download** them.

![Download settings](/screenshots/en/album-settings.png)

## Downloads allowed

If you allow downloads, guests get a download button and receive the **original** file you uploaded.

## Downloads disabled (protected)

Without download permission Wayfare makes saving a photo noticeably harder:

- **Originals are never delivered** — only the display version (max. 1600 px on the long edge).
- Images are delivered through **signed, short-lived URLs**; a copied URL stops working after a short time.
- The browser's context menu, drag-and-drop and long-press "save image" are suppressed, and a transparent overlay sits over each photo.
- Optionally a **watermark** is burned into the delivered image. Set your own watermark text (the album title is the default).

## The honest limits

::: warning Screenshots cannot be prevented
Anything a person can see on a screen can be photographed or captured with a screenshot. No website can stop that. Download protection **raises the bar** against casual saving and bots, but it is not a security guarantee.
:::

If a photo must never leave your circle, don't upload it — or share the album only with a **password** to a small group and use a **watermark**, which at least shows where a leaked copy came from.

## Tips

- Use the watermark for albums shared with large groups.
- Combine with a password and an expiry date for sensitive occasions.
- Locations (GPS) are removed from all delivered versions, whatever you choose.
