# FAQ

### Do my guests need an account?
No, not to **view** an album. Only reacting, commenting and uploading requires signing in.

### Can I change the theme later?
Yes, at any time in the album settings. Photos and comments are unaffected.

### I accidentally shared the link with the wrong person.
Open **Share → Generate new link**. All old links and QR codes stop working immediately. See [Sharing](./sharing).

### How many photos can an album have?
The admin sets a limit per album (default 500) and a maximum size per photo (default 25 MB). Ask your admin if you need more.

### Why are the imported Google photos copied?
Google gives apps only temporary access to selected photos. Copying makes your album independent from your Google account. See [Import](./import).

### Can I stop guests from saving my photos?
You can make it harder (no originals, protected URLs, watermark), but nobody can prevent screenshots. See [Download protection](./download-protection).

### Are photos' GPS locations visible?
No. All delivered versions have their metadata removed.

### Do you track my guests?
No. There are no cookies for analytics, no third-party scripts and no IP storage. See [Notifications & statistics](./notifications-stats).

### Why can't I become an owner?
It depends on the instance's onboarding mode (open, approval, invite). See [Getting started](./getting-started).

### The memorial theme has fewer reactions. Why?
It is intentionally restrained — candles, a white heart, a dove and a rose only.

### Which languages are supported?
German and English.

### Where do I report bugs or ask for features?
On [GitHub](https://github.com/nullthrone/wayfare/issues).
