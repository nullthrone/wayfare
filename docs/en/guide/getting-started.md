# Getting started

## Sign in

Open your Wayfare instance and choose **Sign in**. Depending on what the operator has enabled you can use:

- **Social login** — Google, Apple, Facebook or Discord
- **Magic link** — enter your e-mail address and click the link we send you
- **Passkey** — sign in with fingerprint, face or device PIN (no password)
- **E-mail and password** — if the operator enabled it

![Login page](/screenshots/en/login.png)

Everybody starts as a **viewer**. Viewers can open albums, react and comment, but cannot create albums yet.

## Become an owner

How you become an *owner* depends on the **onboarding mode** the admin picked for the instance:

| Mode | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | A button "Become an owner" | Click it — you are promoted immediately |
| **Approval** | A form "Request owner access" | Write a short message about what you want to share and send it. An admin approves or denies; you will see the status on your dashboard |
| **Invite only** | No button | Ask an admin for an **invite link** (`/invite/<token>`). Opening it while signed in promotes you. Links are valid for 14 days by default and work once |

Invite links work in every mode — an admin can always invite you.

## Your dashboard

After becoming an owner you land on the **dashboard**: all your albums, their status (draft or published), view counts and shortcuts to create a new album.

![Dashboard](/screenshots/en/dashboard.png)

## Your account

Under **Account** you can change your name and picture, see your sign-in methods (add a passkey, for example) and **delete your account**. Deleting removes your albums with all photos, your comments, reactions, connected Google accounts, sessions and passkeys — this cannot be undone.

![Account page](/screenshots/en/account.png)

## Language

Wayfare speaks German and English. The language follows your browser setting and can be switched in the footer or in your account.
