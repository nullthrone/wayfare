# User guide

**Wayfare** lets you share digital photo albums with friends and family the way you would send a postcard: you create an album, pick a theme that fits the occasion, and hand out a **QR code** — on a printed postcard, a sticker, a poster, or as a message. Whoever scans it sees your album in the browser. No app, no download.

![Wayfare landing page](/screenshots/en/landing.png)

## What you can do

- Create **albums** with a cover, greeting text and a theme for the occasion (birthday, wedding, baby, …).
- **Upload** photos or **import** them from Google Photos and Google Drive.
- **Share** via QR code or link, optionally protected by a password and an expiry date — and revoke access at any time.
- **Print** postcards (A6), sticker sheets and posters, or export images for social media stories.
- Let guests **react** and **comment**, and optionally **upload** their own photos — with moderation.
- Protect your photos with **download protection** and a **watermark**.

## Roles

Wayfare has three roles. Each one includes the abilities of the role below it.

| Role | Can do | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewer** (guest) | Open albums you have the link/QR/password for, sign in, react, comment, upload when the owner allows it | Automatically when you sign in |
| **Owner** | Everything a viewer can, plus create and manage your own albums, themes, photo imports, sharing and printing | Depends on the instance's onboarding mode — see [Getting started](./getting-started) |
| **Admin** | Everything an owner can, plus manage users, onboarding, instance settings and public themes | Set up by the operator (the first user or the configured admin e-mail addresses) |
