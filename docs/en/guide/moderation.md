# Moderation & guest uploads

![Moderation](/screenshots/en/album-moderation.png)

## Comment modes

Under **Settings → Comments** choose:

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Publish immediately** | Comments appear right away; you can hide or delete them later |
| **Approve first** | New comments are *pending* until you approve them |
| **Off** | Nobody can comment; existing comments are hidden from guests |

Only signed-in users can comment. Comments are plain text, 1–1000 characters.

## The moderation queue

The **Moderation** tab lists:

- **pending comments** (in *approve first* mode) — approve or hide,
- **reports** — guests can report a comment; you see the reason and the comment and can hide/delete it and mark the report as resolved,
- **pending guest photos** — approve or reject.

Authors can delete their own comments, and you can delete any comment in your albums.

## Blocking viewers

You can block a viewer **for one album**. Blocked users cannot comment, react or upload there, and their existing comments in that album are hidden. You can unblock at any time. Admins can additionally ban users instance-wide.

## Guest uploads

Turn on **Guest uploads** in the settings and set a **limit per guest**. Signed-in guests then see an "Add your photos" button.

- Guest photos are **never** published immediately: they land in the queue as *pending*.
- You get a notification (see [Notifications](./notifications-stats)).
- Approved photos join the album; rejected ones are discarded.

This is perfect for weddings and parties where everyone took pictures.
