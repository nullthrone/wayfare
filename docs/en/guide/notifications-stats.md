# Notifications & statistics

## Notifications

Per album, choose how Wayfare informs you about new comments, guest uploads and reports:

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Off** | No e-mails |
| **Instant** | One e-mail per event |
| **Daily digest** | One summary e-mail per day with everything that happened |

E-mails are sent in your language. They require that the operator configured mail ([operator guide](../operator/mail)); the daily digest additionally needs the operator's scheduled job.

## Statistics

The **Statistics** tab shows, per album:

![Statistics](/screenshots/en/album-stats.png)

- **views** and the time of the last view,
- number of **photos**, **comments** and **reactions**,
- your **top photos** by views and reactions.

### Privacy-friendly by design

Wayfare counts a view **at most once per visitor and day**, using a one-way hash of the browser and day — **no IP addresses are stored, no cookies are set for counting, and no third-party analytics are involved**. The numbers are therefore approximate, which is exactly the point.
