# Photos

![Photo management](/screenshots/en/album-photos.png)

## Upload

Open the album's **Photos** tab and drag photos into the upload area, or tap it to select files (on a phone you can pick from the gallery or take a photo). Uploads run in parallel with a progress bar.

- Supported are common image formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC where the server can decode it).
- The instance has a **maximum size per photo** and a **maximum number of photos per album** — set by the admin. You get a clear message if a photo exceeds them.
- Photos you already imported from Google are skipped when you import them again.

### What happens to your photos

For every photo Wayfare creates a small *thumbnail* and a *display version* (max. 1600 px). These versions have **all metadata removed — including GPS location**. The untouched original is kept and only handed out if you allow downloads.

## Captions

Click a photo and add a **caption**. Captions are shown in the lightbox in the viewer.

## Cover photo

Choose **Set as cover** on a photo. The cover is shown on the album's opening page, on the postcard front and on posters.

## Order

Drag photos to reorder them. The order is saved automatically and is the order guests see.

## Delete

Select one or more photos and delete them. Comments and reactions on a deleted photo are removed too. If the deleted photo was the cover, pick a new one.

## Guest uploads

Photos uploaded by guests wait in the **moderation queue** until you approve or reject them — see [Moderation](./moderation).
