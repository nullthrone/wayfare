# Printing

Go to the **Share** tab → **Print & export**. Wayfare generates ready-to-print PDFs and images in your album's theme colours.

![Print & export](/screenshots/en/album-share.png)

## Formats

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| **Postcard A6** | 2-page PDF: front with cover photo, title and theme ornament; back with greeting, QR code (at least 30 mm) and short URL. 105 × 148 mm plus 3 mm bleed with crop marks |
| **Stickers** | A4 sheet with 12 stickers (3 × 4), 60 mm each, **round** or **square** |
| **Poster A4 / A3** | Title, greeting excerpt and a large QR code on the theme's background colour |
| **Social story** | 1080 × 1920 px image (PNG or SVG) for Instagram/WhatsApp stories |
| **Social square** | 1080 × 1080 px image (PNG or SVG) |
| **QR code only** | Bare QR code as SVG or PNG |

::: info Fonts and emojis in PDFs
PDF text uses standard fonts: umlauts and accents work, but emojis are removed from the printed text.
:::

## Printing tips

For the postcard (A6):

- **Paper:** 250–300 g/m² photo or postcard card stock; matte or silk finish keeps the QR code readable.
- **Bleed:** the PDF already contains **3 mm bleed** and crop marks. In a copy shop, ask for a "cut to crop marks" / "trim to size" job. If you print at home, you can ignore the bleed and trim along the marks.
- **No scaling:** print at **100 % / "actual size"**. Turn off "fit to page" in the print dialog — scaling makes the card too small and the QR code harder to scan.
- **Duplex:** choose double-sided, *flip on short edge* (portrait) so the back is oriented correctly.
- **Drugstore photo services:** upload the PDF as a custom-size print (105 × 148 mm) or export the front as an image. Check that the service doesn't auto-crop edges.
- **Test first:** print one copy and scan it with a phone before you print 50.

For stickers use A4 sticker paper (matt, white); the sheet layout is 3 × 4, so use label paper without a pre-printed grid or one that matches 60 mm labels. Print at 100 %.

For posters, an A4/A3 print on normal paper or at a copy shop is enough. Make sure the QR code is at least 3 cm wide and scannable from the viewing distance (roughly 10:1).

## Social formats

The story and square images are meant for sharing online: post them to your story or send them in a chat. The QR code works from a screen, too.

## Re-print after a revocation

After you [generate a new link](./sharing#revoke-and-regenerate), old exports no longer work. Export again.
