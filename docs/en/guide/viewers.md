# For guests (viewers)

Somebody sent you a Wayfare card or link? Here is what to expect.

## Opening an album

Scan the **QR code** with your phone's camera or tap the link. The album opens in your browser — no app needed.

![Album cover](/screenshots/en/viewer-cover.png)

If the album is **password protected**, you'll see a lock screen. Ask the sender for the password.

![Password screen](/screenshots/en/viewer-password.png)

If you see "expired" or "not found", the owner has ended or renewed the share — ask for a new link.

## Browsing

Scroll through the photo grid, tap a photo to open it full screen and swipe or use the arrow keys to move on.

![Photo grid](/screenshots/en/viewer-grid.png)

![Lightbox](/screenshots/en/viewer-lightbox.png)

## Signing in

To **react, comment or upload** you must sign in — with Google, Apple, Facebook, Discord, a magic link in your e-mail, a passkey or e-mail/password, depending on what the instance offers. Viewing doesn't require an account.

## Reacting and commenting

Tap one of the emoji under a photo to react (tap again to remove it). Write a comment in the lightbox; depending on the album it appears immediately or after the owner approved it. You can delete your own comments and report inappropriate ones.

![Comments](/screenshots/en/viewer-comments.png)

## Uploading your own photos

If the owner enabled guest uploads you'll see an **Add photos** button. Your photos appear after the owner approved them. There may be a limit per guest.

## Downloading

A download button only exists if the owner allowed it. Otherwise photos are view-only.

## Privacy

- Wayfare uses **no tracking and no third-party services** in your browser; even fonts are served by the instance itself.
- Views are counted anonymously, without storing your IP address.
- Locations (GPS) in photos have been removed.
- If you signed in, the owner sees your name next to your comments, reactions and uploads. You can delete your account any time under **Account**, which also removes your comments and reactions.
